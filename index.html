Why Mentoobeing

Sometimes it's not one thing. It's everything at once.

Student life can bring academic pressure, career uncertainty, comparison, relationships, loneliness, family expectations, money worries, sleep problems and an always-on digital world.

Understand

Put words to what has been taking up space in your mind and everyday life.

Reflect

Notice patterns without turning them into labels or diagnoses.

Take one small step

Choose something practical you can try today, instead of trying to fix your whole life.

What it is

Mentoobeing isn't here to tell you what's wrong with you.

It's a student-first space for checking in with yourself. Your answers are meant to help you notice what may be weighing on you and what you might want to pay attention to.

Wellbeing Guide

Don't fix your whole life. Start with one thing.

Eight practical areas. Pick the one that feels relevant instead of trying to do everything at once.

01

Get Out of Your Head

Go outside, slow down and notice what's around you.

02

Move Your Body

Walk, cycle, stretch, play, dance or train. Something is better than nothing.

03

Fix Your Sleep, Not Your Whole Life

Work on a realistic sleep routine and identify what keeps getting in the way.

04

Take Back Control of Your Phone

Notice your triggers and choose one boundary that feels doable.

05

Understand Your Thoughts & Emotions

Separate what happened, what you feel, what you tell yourself and what you know.

06

Don't Handle Everything Alone

Make space for honest conversations, support and healthy boundaries.

07

Deal With Pressure, Not Your Entire Future

Sort things into NOW, NEXT and NOT IN MY CONTROL.

08

Be On Your Own Side

Notice harsh self-talk and ask whether you'd speak to a friend that way.
